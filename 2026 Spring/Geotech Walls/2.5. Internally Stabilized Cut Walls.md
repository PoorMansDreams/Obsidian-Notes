Design Procedure of Soil-Nail Walls

* Load definition
* Soil-nail configuration and material selection
* Selection of factors of safety is ASD (or resistance factors id. LRFD)
* Evaluation of internal stability (reinforcement rupture, reinforcement pullout, and facing)
* Evaluation of external stability (sliding and global)
* Evaluation of lateral and vertical displacements

![[Pasted image 20260423150607.png]]

There are many intermediate steps, to ensure that each layer is safe as we continue constructing or cutting. 

There are "pullout" forces on the nail, or tensile forces. There are two pieces on the nail. The active piece, which is the left side, and the passive piece which is the right side. The right side of the nail is soil pulling on the nail (to the right). While the left side is where the soil pulls the nail to the left. 

We want to ensure that the nail stays in the wall, and does not get pulled out by the "active" soil.
