There are different ways a wall can fail as well,
- Sliding
	- The whole wall slides horizontally
- Overturning
	- The whole wall tips over on on the bottom corner
- Overstressing
	- the wall rotates on the toe, one corner goes down and the opposite end corner goes up
- Global Failure 
	- The wall rotates around the stem or somewhere along the top of the wall.
