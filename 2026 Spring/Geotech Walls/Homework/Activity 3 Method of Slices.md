$$

$$
\text{FS}=\frac{\sum M_{r}}{\sum M_{D}}= \frac{82752.91 \frac{\text{lb-ft}}{\text{ft}}}{483305.9 \frac{\text{lb-ft}}{\text{ft}}}=0.171
$$
