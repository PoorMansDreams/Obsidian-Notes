1. Plot a profile for the net pore-water pressure, u, (net lateral pressure) acting against the
embedded part of the sheet pile wall shown in the figure below if the difference in the upstream
and downstream water surface elevations is 10 feet.

![[Pasted image 20260204161450.png]]
Sample Calculations
$$
\begin{align} 
\Delta h&=\frac{H}{n_{d}}=\frac{10}{18}=0.556\\
H_{a}&=H-1\Delta h \\
H_{a}&=10-1\left( \frac{10}{18} \right)=9.39\text{ ft} \\
\frac{u}{\gamma_{w}}&=H_{a}-z=10.89\text{ ft} \\
u&=10.89\text{ ft}\times 62.4 \frac{\text{lb}}{\text{ft}^3}=679.47 \frac{\text{ lb}}{\text{ft}^2}
\end{align}

$$
