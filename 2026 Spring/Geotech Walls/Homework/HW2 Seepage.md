Sample Calculations
$$
\begin{align} 
\Delta h&=\frac{H}{n_{d}}=\frac{11}{18}\\
H_{a}&=H-1\Delta h \\
H_{a}&=10-1\left( \frac{11}{18} \right)=9.39\text{ ft} \\
\frac{u}{\gamma_{w}}&=H_{a}-z=10.89\text{ ft} \\
u&=10.89\text{ ft}\times 62.4 \frac{\text{lb}}{\text{ft}^3}=679.47 \frac{\text{ lb}}{\text{ft}^2}
\end{align}
