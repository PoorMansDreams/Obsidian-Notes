#### Location
The slope is located east of the intersection between the Garden Grove Freeway bridge, and Beach Blvd. Coordinates for it are 33°46'14.9"N 117°59'31.7"W

#### Type of Structure
The soil serves as a abutment for the freeway bridge 
#### Classification
The slope seems to be a fill, as there is a valley west of the slope shown. 
#### Height
About 20' of soil from the ground to the top of the slope. 
#### Surface Cover
Directly under the bridge the surface is covered with concrete, while the area around it is covered in grass, small shrubs, and small trees.
#### Structures in the Vicinity
The Garden Grove Freeway that goes over Beach Blvd. There are also ramps to exit or enter the freeway here.
#### Conditions of the Slope
The slope looks perfectly fine, there doesn't appear to be any significant damage to the slope, the concrete under the bridge and on top of the concrete seems to be fine as well. No noticeable cracks or deformations can be seen. There are small trees planted on the slope, but I don't think they're large enough to make a impact to the soil's integrity.
