
* Not suitable for soil deposits containing gravel, cobbles, or boulders (give refusal and damage equipment)
* Driving a hollow thick-walled tube into the ground
* Measuring the number of blows to advance a split-spoon 1 ft
* A drop weight system is used (Wh=140 lbs; Hd=2.5 ft) repeatedly to achieve 3 successive increments 6" each.
* The 1st increment is seating load, the sum of the latter two increments is called N-value (blow count) or SPT resistance

We keep striking the rod into the soil, however we stop when

*  Sampler advanced to complete 1.5 ft without limiting blow counts
* A total of 50 blows have been applied during one of the 3 increments
* A total of 100 blows have been applied
* No observed advance during application of 10 successive blows
* Sampler sinks under the weight of rods (WOR) or rods and hammer (WOH)

Theoretical SPT energy (dropping of the hammer)

Potential Energy,
$$
PE=W_{h}\cdot H_{d}=140\text{ lb}\cdot 2.5'
=350\text{ lb-ft}
$$
Theoretical kinetic energy 
$$
KE_{T}=\frac{1}{2}m_{h}\cdot v_{h}^2=\frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{w_{h}}{g} \right)(\sqrt{ 2g\cdot H_{d} })^2=W_{h}\cdot H_{d}
$$

due to efficiency, losses are inevitable

$$
E_{m}=ER_{m}=\frac{KE_{A}}{KE_{T}}
$$

Hammer efficiency is commonly around $E_{m}=30-60\%$
An auto hammer efficiency is commonly around $E_{m}=70-90\%$
We will have to have a hammer efficiency correction. which is
$$
C_{E}=\frac{E_{m}}{60\%}
$$
---
Rod Length Correction

$$
C_{R}\text{: Rod length correction factor}
$$
As the hammer strikes the rod it transmit compression waves through the body of rod, those waves will interfere with the following compression waves from the following hammer strike and lower energy is transmitted at the bottom. In short, hitting the rod causes it to vibrate, and the energy is being lost. Longer rod causes less energy lost. It is a function based on the length of the rod

$$
C_{R}=fn(L_{R})
$$

| $L_{R}$ | $C_{R}$ |
| ------- | ------- |
| <3m     | 0.75    |
| 3-4m    | 0.80    |
| 4-6m    | 0.85    |
| 6-10m   | 0.95    |
| 10-30m  | 1.00    |
