We use The Unified Soil Classification System, or the USCS
The factors that we use to find or classify each soil is the soil particle's diameter, and how much there is  of it. In short, Gradation, and **Sieve Analysis**[^1]. Sieve Analysis usually only sieves until #200, which is 0.075mm hole diameter. From then on we have to use **hydrometer analysis**[^2]

[^1]: pass soil through multiple sieves with varying different hole sizes. Soil will be retained and whatnot

[^2]: Particles will be put into water, larger particles will sink faster, while smaller finer particles will sink slower.
