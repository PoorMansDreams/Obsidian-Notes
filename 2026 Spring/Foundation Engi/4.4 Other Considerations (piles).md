What can happen is that soft clays and compressible soils may exert negative friction on piles. The negative skin friction is calculated like skin resistance but it goes downwards instead of upwards. 

This can be seen as $\gamma_{NS}F_{NS}$ where gamma is the LOAD FACTOR. the negative skin friction is denoted as $F_{NS}$, it is put into our equation as part of the Factored $P_{column}$ . 

In short, the clay compresses, and it tries to drag the pile with it (downward).

**NOTE** if there is a dense sand (that doesn't compress) on top of the soft clay, the dense sand will be dragged down by the soft clay as well. (Both will have negative skin friction)
