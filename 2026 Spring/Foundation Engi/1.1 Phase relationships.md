
Even though we use soils as our only way to put our structures on, they are highly variable. soil properties are hard to change, complex, and the boundaries are not well defined. In short, there are many grey zones and somewhat vague soil properties. It is also difficult to find what specific soils are there underneath the earth.

The Phase diagram is quite expansive, but can be explained here

Porosity: how much air/water volume is in the soil compared to the total volume

Void Ratio: how much air/water is in the soil compared to the solids 

Specific Gravity: Specific weight of the solids compared to the specific weight of water

Degree of saturation: Volume of water compared to the volume of air/water 

Gravimetric Water Content: weight of water compared to weight of solids

Volumetric water content: how much volume of water compared to total volume

Bulk Unit weight: Weight of the soil compared to total volume

Saturated Unit weight: The weight of water and solids compared to total volume 

Dry unit weight: weight of the solids compared to total volume of the soil

Submerged Unit Weight: Unit weight when the soil is completely submerged in water (Unit weight of saturated soil subtracted by the unit weight of water)

Unit weight = Specific Weight 

for any saturation, we can find the volumetric water content by multiplying porosity by Saturation
