Knowing the formula $S=m_{v}\cdot \Delta \sigma \cdot H$. We can also use a classic formula from deformable materials.
$$
\Delta L=\frac{PL}{EA} 
$$
This is for immediate settlement from the **Pile itself**. The pile will very slightly deform (compress most likely) 

For driven piles, there is already slipping between the skin of the pile and the surrounding soil. By the time the pile has been fully driven, the soil has already mobilized (settled). But will still need a very small 1% for end displacement

For drilled shafts, the pile will need about 0.15 inches for the side soil to mobilize. But we haven't pressed much against the end of the pile with the soil under the pile. It takes about 5-10% of the diameter of the pile will mobilize (settle).

The soil however will compress much more.
