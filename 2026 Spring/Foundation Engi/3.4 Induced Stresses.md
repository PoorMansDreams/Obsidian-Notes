
Definition: induced stress is the change in stress within soil due to the application of a new load.

Net bearing stress, $q_{\text{applied,net}}$ 
induced stress $\Delta \sigma_{z}=I_{z}\cdot q_{\text{applied,net}}$

$\Delta \sigma$ will always be less than our bearing stress. It can **Never** feel negative stress, nor stress equal or more than the net bearing stress $q_{\text{applied,net}}$ 
Our range for $I_{z}$ is from 0-1. Induced stresses refers to the CHANGE of soil stress, (since the soil is already under stress due to itself). Induced stress is the change of stress from the pre-existing stress. 

in short, $\Delta \sigma=\sigma_{\text{after}}-\sigma_{\text{before}}$

As we move deeper the stresses is less, at the top, the soil feels 100% of the stresses.
