This usually happens when the interaction areas (between the soil and the pile) intercept each other. In short, if two piles moves, the soil may move with the pile. It is as if the entire area where the pile group is, moves as one. 

This is not very good, as that means that the soil is not giving any "skin resistance" to the pile.
