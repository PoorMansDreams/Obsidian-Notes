Stresses in a soil comes from two things
* Stress from geostatic stresses (stress from self weight)
* Induced Stress (stress from a load like a house)
The deeper you go, the more stress the soil experiences.

![[Foundation  Ex1]]
There are two stresses, total stress and effective stress, $\sigma$ and $\sigma'$, we will be using effective stress, which is the true stress. Total stress ignores the weight of water. Effective stress worries about the weight change due to water.
