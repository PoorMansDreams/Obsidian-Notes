1. Estimate bulk weight in pcf, 
Water content $\omega =55\%=0.55$
SG = 2.7, S=1, since the sample is below groundwater table
$$
\gamma_{b}=\frac{W_{t}}{V_{t}}=\frac{W_{w}+W_{s}}{V_{v}+V_{s}}=\frac{0.55+1}{\frac{0.55}{62.4\times 1}+\frac{1}{2.7\times 62.4}}=105.088 \frac{\text{lb}}{\text{ft}^3}
$$

2. Estimate Dry unit weight
$$
\gamma_{d}=\frac{W_{s}}{V_{t}}=\frac{1}{\frac{0.55}{62.4\times 1}+\frac{1}{2.7\times 62.4}}=67.798 \frac{\text{lb}}{\text{ft}^3}
$$
3. Estimate e (void ratio)
$$
e=\frac{V_{v}}{V_{s}}=\frac{\frac{W_{w}}{62.4\times S}}{\frac{1}{\text{G.S.}\times 62.4}}=\frac{W_{w}\times\text{G.S.}}{S}=\frac{0.55\times2.7}{1}=1.485
$$
