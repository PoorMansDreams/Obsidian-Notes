
1. A footing supports a 1.25 ft x 1.25 ft column that carries 400 kips (dead load) and 100 kips (live load). The foundation level is 4.5 ft below the ground surface. The gross allowable bearing capacity is 4 ksf. The unit of soil is 120 pcf. You are required to provide complete design calculations for bearing, shear, and flexure for the footing. Assume  fc = 4 ksi, fy = 60 ksi. Calculate B

$$
q_{allowed}=\frac{P_{T}}{B^2}+\sigma_{v}
$$

$P_T$ is deadload + live load, don't use load factors. 

$$
4 \frac{\text{k}}{\text{ft}^2}=\frac{400 \text{ kip}+100 \text{ kip}}{B^2}+120 \frac{\text{lb}}{\text{ft}^3}\times \frac{1 \text{ kip}}{1000\text{ lb}}\times 4.5\text{ ft}
$$

$$
B=12
$$
