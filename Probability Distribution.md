We can describe a probability distribution mathematically using a probability mass function, f(x)
$$
f(x_{i})=P(X=x_{i})
$$
simply means that the value of the function equals the probability
$$
f(x_{i})\geq 0
$$
means the probability cant be negative
$$
\sum^n_{i=1}f(x_{i})=1
$$
total probability must be 1, 

Mean - measure of the center or middle of the probability distribution

Variance - measure of the dispersion or variability of the distribution (often referred to as the second moment)

Mean can be show as $\mu=E(X)=\sum_{x}xf(x)$
if the question uses the word "expected" or "expect", you're calculating for the mean for sure. 

Variance can be denoted as $\sigma^2=V(X)=\sum_{x}(x-\mu)^2f(x)$
and alternate equation for variance is also $\sum_{x}x^2(fx)-\mu^2$

The standard deviation is denotes as $\sigma$
