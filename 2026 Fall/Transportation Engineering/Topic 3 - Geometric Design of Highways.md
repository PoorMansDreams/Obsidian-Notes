### Design Speed
- A selected speed to determine various geometric design features of the roadway
- An engineer should select a design speed appropriate to assure that drivers can travel at their desired speeds, other factors include
	- Terrain, traffic volume, traffic composition, costs of right-of-way and construction, aesthetic consideration
- Ranging from 15 to 75 mph in increments of 5 mph (15, 20, …, 70, 75 mph). Change in design speed should be made over a sufficient distance

### Design Vehicle
- How vehicle characteristics affect highway design:
	- Vehicle width $\to$ lane width
	- Vehicle length $\to$ roadway capacity, turning radius
	- Vehicle height $\to$ the clearances of structures
	- Vehicle weight $\to$ structural design
	- Vehicle acceleration/deceleration $\to$ highway alignment
- AASHTO recommends four design vehicle classes:
1. Passenger cars
2. Buses
3. Trucks
4. Recreational Vehicles
- Select the largest vehicle that’s expected to use the roadway in significant numbers on a daily basis during the design year.
- Limit the speed differential between passenger cars and trucks to a max. of 10 mph. (upgrade, length of the upgrade

### Limit-Access Highways
- Most expressways or freeways in US belong to this category
- Limit access to improve highway capacity
- Desirable spacing of access points or interchanges
- 1 mile in urban areas
- 3 ~ 5 miles in rural areas
