#Magic

How to define the differences between Magic, sorcery, witchcraft? 

### Magic

One definition of magic is that there is a belief that a person is helped by supernatural powers can directly influence particular outcomes.

Magic could also be pseudo-science, which is a confused cause and effect Ex. using specific herbs would help heal someone. 

**My interpretation of magic**; probably being able to do something that cannot be simply explained through any meaningful scientific way. Something that has NEVER been seen before or done.  

- Michel Foucault notes that people in power can say one's religion is magic/witchcraft. Dominant groups are able to construct what "magic" and "witchcraft" are to assert power. In short, one person's religion is another's magic. 
- unsanctioned power or power of the marginalized group of people have a form of power

### Witchcraft

- Multiple meanings of witch and witchcraft 
- It means the Wiccan tradition (don't use power to harm people, closer to more like a druid) (Nature)
- Indigenous religion of Europe
- Usually used to accuse someone of being a deviant "something negative" example: goth girl is witchy
- "Witches" are often scapegoats for social tension. 
- For some, Witchcraft is a way to channel supernatural forces for a variety of agendas (CASTING A HEX ON YOU)
- European image of a witch: colonized people who practiced indigenous religions
- Globally, some women's non-orthodox practices have been called witchcraft 
- Yet, Navaho culture, various African cultures, and other groups as well do have their own indigenous concept of witchcraft
