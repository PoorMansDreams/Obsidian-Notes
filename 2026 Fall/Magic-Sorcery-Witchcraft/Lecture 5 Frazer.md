#Magic
James George Frazer - (1845-1941) Social Anthropologist, Glasgow, Scotland, inspired by Tylor

Had a similar evolution theory. Famous for *The Golden Bough* which is in 12 volumes, armchair anthropologist (never went out) 

---
Tylor's model was Magic $\rightarrow$ Religion $\rightarrow$  Science

Frazer believed that Religion is simply refined magic, magic is early science, All 3 based on similar intellectual reasoning. 

His works has to do with the connections between power, magic, taboos, death, violence, and fertility. There's always some kind of similar myths. 

Frazer believed that there's two kinds of magic. 
- Sympathetic magic - Similar 
	- Copying something to kind of do something, like a rain dance. Mimicking the rain in a form of dance to summon rain. (Voodoo doll, make something that looks like someone to do stuff to them)
- Contagious magic (contact)
	- Because you are touching someone's hair, you have some power over it. Example, rabbit's foot (lucky) sticking something close to you for luck

Religion has the conception of personal agents, (deity's and gods). Australian aborigines are the most primitive. Believed that Magic was the product of "faulty logic of ignorant and dull-witted people everywhere". 

Said that magic was a form of protoscience, believed that magic was a early form of science.

---

Frazer's Golden Bough;

Goddess Diana embodied in a sacred tree, maybe an oak. Knock on wood means to knock on the wood of a tree to awaken a spirit within the tree. 

A golden bough grows on this tree, Diana "haunts" her grove.

A grim figure protects the forests with his sword. He is a priest, known as King of The Woods. Will be murdered by his incumbent, who wants to pluck this golden bough.

This bough is between heaven earth, There must be something special about you for you to be able to take this golden bough. You have to have some kind of violence to save life. (life for death, death for life)

Frazer believes that Prophets, Priests, Magicians, Kings, were all the same thing. Priesthood and King was common. Believed that magicians as well as priests were often kings among primitive people. Attained power by "magic" to be king. 

Most kings kind of dress like a wizard. Believed that ALL RELIGIONS ARE FERTILITY CULTS, that feature the worship and sacrifice of a sacred King/Prophet.

In the end, thinks that magical thinking should evolve into religious thinking and then into scientific thinking

Believed science is faith, (believes in the 5 senses). Believes that there is an order in belief.

Frazer thinks that science is nothing but theories of thought. Believed that there might be something beyond science?
