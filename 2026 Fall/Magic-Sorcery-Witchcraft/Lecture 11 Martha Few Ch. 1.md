#Magic
Women who lived "evil lives" 

Few looks at the power dynamics between caste, race, gender in colonial Guatemala. 

* Studies marginalized women from 17th to 18th century Guatemala. 
* Her sources are inquisition and colonial documents from that time period
	* Consider that in these sources, the writer is probably incredibly biased. 

Story of Padre Jose de Quevedo, denunciation of Lorenza de Molina and her sister in law 

Padre claimed that these two women came in the form of a ball of light transformed back into women, then overpowered the priest, strip them, put them on a cross, upside-down. Then they start hurting his genitalia, and make a pact with the devil, the priest then awakens with wounds all over his body.

Marginalized women wielded degrees of agency through knowledge of healing and spells, People from all the distinct layers of the community would consult them for healing, disputes, relationships, etc. 

These witch-women were actually consulted by others (they were empowered). The colonizers, in short, believed they really could do what they do.
