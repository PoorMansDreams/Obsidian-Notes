#transport 
**Example 1**

A weekly volume of goods is transported between plant and major port is 1000 containers, there are three possible modes available to the shipper, truck, rail, and water.

The seaport is U=0.05C + 0.1T where C is cost $/ton and T is total door-to door travel time

Truck delivers 30 metric tons per 16 hours 
Rail deliver 17 metric tons per 25 hours
Water delivers 12 metric tons per 30 hours
30/16 = 1.88 mt/hr
17/25 = 0.68 mt/hr
12/30 = 0.40 mt/hr

utility value for each node 
U_t=(0.05x30)+(0.10x16) = -3.10
U_r=(0.05x17)+(0.10x25) = -3.35
U_w=(0.05x12)+(0.10x30)=-3.60
