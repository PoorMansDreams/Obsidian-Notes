#transportation

• The Kyoto Protocol identifies six  
gases for emission reduction  
targets:  
– carbon dioxide (CO2 ),  
– methane (CH 4 ),  
– nitrous oxide (N2 O),  
– sulfur hexafluoride (SF 6 ),  
– hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), and  
– perfluorocarbons (PFCs).  
– ARB's emission inventory  
includes estimates of California  
emissions of these six gases,  
plus nitrogen trifluoride (NF 3 ).
