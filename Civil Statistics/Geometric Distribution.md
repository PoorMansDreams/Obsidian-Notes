$$
f(x)=(1-p)^{x-1}p
$$
We can use this if each trial is independent,
each trial only has 2 outcomes, 
and the probability of success in each trial, $p$ is constant,
then we have a Bernoulli Trial

simple way to denote this is 
$$
\mu=E(X)=\frac{1}{p}
$$
note that if we have $p=0.2$ we **expect** to take $\frac{1}{0.2}=5$ attempts until we succeed at our task.
