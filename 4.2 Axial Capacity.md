Friction has to do with the force normal to the side of the pile, in this case, the only force normal to the side is the **Lateral Earth Pressure**. 

NOTE, $\phi'$ is the INTERNAL angle of friction, usually in soils, This is the cohesion with itself. 
$\phi_{f}$ is the adhesion, the ability for the soil to stick to the structure (The Pile)

The friction force between the structure and soil is shown as 
$$
\begin{align}
f_{n}=\tan \phi_{f}\times k\times \sigma'_{v} \\
=\tan\left( \phi'\times \frac{\phi_{f}}{\phi'} \right)\times \left( k_{o}\times \frac{k}{k_{o}} \right)\times \sigma_{v}'
\end{align}
$$
$k$ may change due to high/low displacement due to driving or drilling,
For the ratios for $\phi$ and $k$ we typically use values from a table.
