 #environmental
* The state where the concentration of all reactants and products remain constant with time.  
* On the molecular level, there is frantic activity. Equilibrium is not static but is a highly dynamic situation.  
Example: the flow of cars across a bridge connecting two islands  
Assuming the traffic flow on the bridge is the same in both directions.

The equilibrium constant $K$ expresses the relationship between products and reactants of a reaction at equilibrium

$$
4\text{NH}_3(\text{g}) + 7\text{O}_2(\text{g}) \rightleftharpoons 4\text{NO}_2(\text{g}) + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}(\text{g})
$$
$$
K=\frac{[NO_{2}]^4[H_{2}O]^6}{[NH_{3}]^4[O_{2}]^7}
$$
The products are on top while the reactants are at the bottom. The coefficient is shown as a exponent in the K equation. 

Q is the initial concentration, which means that the reaction might shift, 
If Q = K, then there's no shift.

$$
\text{N}_2(\text{g}) + 3\text{H}_2(\text{g}) \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NH}_3(\text{g})
$$
If Q > K, then there is more product than there is reactant, (there is too much product). The equation will shift to the left

and If Q < K, then there is more reactant than there is product. The system will shift to the right.
$$
Q = \frac{[\text{NH}_3]_0^2}{[\text{N}_2]_0[\text{H}_2]_0^3}
$$

###### Example: Equilibrium
$$
2{NO}(\text{g}) \rightleftharpoons \text{N}_2(\text{g}) + \text{O}_2(\text{g})
$$

Knowing that K = $2.4\times10^3$
$$
Q=\frac{[N_{2}]_{0}[O_{2}]_{0}}{[NO]^2_{0}}
$$
a) 2.0L flask contains 0.032 mol NO, 0.62 mol N 2, and 4.0 mol O 2 .  
$$
\frac{\left[ \frac{0.62}{2} \right]_{0}\left[ \frac{4}{2} \right]_{0}}{\left[ \frac{0.032}{2} \right]^2_{0}} = 2421=Q
$$
This is pretty close to K already, so we can say that it is at equilibrium

b) 3.0L flask contains 0.060 mol NO, 2.4 mol N 2, and 1.7 mol O 2.
$$
\frac{\left[ \frac{2.4}{3} \right]_{0}\left[ \frac{1.7}{3} \right]_{0}}{\left[ \frac{0.06}{3} \right]^2_{0}} = 1125=Q
$$
In this case, Q < K, so then the equation will shift to the right, towards the product side

$$
