#### Limiting Reactants
A reaction can happen, but there could be an excess of reactants, leading to one reactant being consumed, but the other not being completely consumed, we can solve this out by calculating which reactant will give the least amount of mols of product. 

$$
2 \text{Al}(\text{s}) + \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3(\text{s}) \rightarrow \text{Al}_2\text{O}_3(\text{s}) + 2\text{Fe}(\text{l})
$$
How many grams of molten Fe can be formed from a mixture of 30g Al and 90g $Fe_2O_3$
$$\frac{30g}{1} \times \frac{1}{27g}\times \frac{2}{2}\times \frac{56g}{1}=62.22g$$

$$\frac{90g}{1} \times \frac{1}{160g}\times \frac{2}{1}\times \frac{56g}{1}=63g$$
The limiting reactant is the aluminum because it would give 62.22g of Fe before it runs out.
