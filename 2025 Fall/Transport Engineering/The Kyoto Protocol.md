• The Kyoto Protocol identifies six  
gases for emission reduction  
targets:  
– carbon dioxide (CO2 ),  
– methane (CH 4 ),  
– nitrous oxide (N2 O),  
– sulfur hexafluoride (SF 6 ),  
– hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), and  
– perfluorocarbons (PFCs).  
– ARB's emission inventory  
includes estimates of California  
emissions of these six gases,  
plus nitrogen trifluoride (NF 3 ).

To meet the Kyoto Protocol, we need at lease 11,000 lb of CO2/Year/Person, or -7% from 1990. We want to limit personal carbon emissions to 11,000 pounds of CO2/year

This is equivalent to
- Fly coast to coast twice on economy
- Drive 11,000 miles, (20 mpg)
- Use 16 cubic yards of concrete
- Use 14 cubic feet of steel
- Use 5 cubic feet of aluminum
This is because it takes time to drive these materials. 
1/3rd of the CO2 emissions is from personal decisions, the rest is due to services and industry.
