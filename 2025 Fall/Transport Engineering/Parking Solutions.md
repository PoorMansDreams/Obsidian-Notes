A curbed section of a two-lane road passes through an urban area. The section is 1.2 mi long and 40 ft wide curb to curb. Each lane is 11 ft wide. Parking is allowed on both sides of the road. The traffic consists mostly of passenger cars. An on-street parking configuration that minimizes interference with traffic movement is desired. What is most nearly the maximum number of parking spaces on this section of road?

$$
W_{p}=\frac{W-2W_{L}}{2}=\frac{40-2(11)}{2}=9\text{ ft}
$$
With 9 feet of width this is only enough for parallel parking. 
number of cars that can fit in this strip of road is $N$
$$
N=\frac{2\text{ (number of sides)}(1.2\text{ mi})\left( \frac{5280\text{ ft}}{\text{ 1 mi}} \right)}{22 \frac{\text{ ft}}{\text{space}}}=576\text{ spaces}
$$
