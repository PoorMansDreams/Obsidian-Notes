#probability 

For one sided distribution, gives us $t$
`t.inv(probability, degree of freedom)`

For two sided distributions, give us t
`t.inv.2t(probability, degree of freedom)`

Gives us the two-sided confidence interval
`confidence.t(alpha, stnddev, sampsize)`

For left-tailed distribution (p value) 
`t.dist(x, degree of freedom, Cumulative)`

For right-tailed distribution (p value)
`t.dist.rt(x, degree of freedom)`

For a two-tailed distribution, (p value)
`t.dist.2t(x, degree of freedom)`

For a two sided distribution, this will give us the p value directly. 
`t.test(array1, array2, 2, 3`
