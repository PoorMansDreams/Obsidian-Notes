#probability 
For a continuous probability distribution, the area under a curve should be 1.

if the base times height of a rectangle is equal to 1 then,
$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{b-a}
$$
which then gives us 
$$
P(X\leq x)=\int ^x_{a} \frac{1}{b-a}du=\frac{x-a}{b-a}
$$
the answer will be 1 if $x>b$ 
the probability will be 0 if $x<a$
