Mirex (MW = 540) is a fully chlorinated organic pesticide that was manufactured to control fire ants. Due to its structure, mirex is very unreactive; thus, it persists in the environment. Lake Erie water samples have had mirex measured as high as 0.002 μg/L and trout samples with 0.002 μg/g

1. In the water samples, what is the aqueous concentration of mirex in unit of ppt?
$$
 \frac{1\mu\text{g}}{\text{L}}=\frac{1000\text{ ng}}{\text{L}}=1000\text{ ppt}
$$
$$
\frac{0.002\mu \text{g}}{\text{L}}\times \frac{1000\text{ ng}}{1\mu \text{g}}=\frac{2\text{ ng}}{L}=\boxed{2\text{ ppt}}
$$
2. In the fish samples, what is the concentration of mirex in ppm?
$$
 \frac{1\text{ mg}}{1 \text{ kg}}=1\text{ ppm}
$$
$$
\frac{0.002\mu \text{g}}{\text{g}}\times \frac{1\text{ mg}}{1000\mu \text{g}}\times \frac{1000\text{ g}}{1\text{ kg}}=\frac{0.002\text{ mg}}{1\text{ kg}}=\boxed{0.002\text{ ppm}}
$$
