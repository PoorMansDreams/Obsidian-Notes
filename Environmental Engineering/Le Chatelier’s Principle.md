#chemistry
a) Concentration case: changing the concentration of a chemical will shift the equilibrium to the side that would reduce that change in concentration  

N 2 + 3H 2 ↔ 2NH3  

b) Temperature case: when temperature is increased, the heat content of the system increases, so the system would consume some of that heat by shifting that equilibrium to either forward (endo) or reverse (exo) direction  

A + B ↔ AB + heat (∆H=negative value, exothermic)  
heat + C + D ↔ CD (∆H=positive value, endothermic)

Pressure case: a change in pressure due to a change in volume of the system will shift the equilibrium counteracting the pressure increase by shifting to the side with fewer moles of gas that exert less pressure  

N2 + 3H2 ↔ 2NH3  
(4 moles) ↔ (2 moles)

**anything changing with SOLIDS or LIQUIDS will not change equilibrium much.**
