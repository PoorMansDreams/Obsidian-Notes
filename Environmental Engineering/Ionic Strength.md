Ionic strength can be calculated by using
$$
I=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i}  (C_{i}Z_{i}^2)
$$
C is concentration and Z is charge

so for 0.15 M of Na$_{3}$PO$_4$ 
Na$_{3}$PO$_4$ = 3Na$^{+1}$ + PO$_4^{-3}$

$$
\frac{1}{2}[3\cdot 0.15\cdot 1^2+0.15\cdot 3^2]=0.9 \text{ M}
$$
the times 3 is due to the 3 Na, and there's only 1 PO4
