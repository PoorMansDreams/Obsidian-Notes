#laws
Prop 65, officially the **Safe Drinking Water and Toxic Enforcement Act of 1986**, is a California law intended to protect the state's drinking water sources from being contaminated with chemicals known to cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm, and to require businesses to inform the public about exposures to such chemicals.

|Action/Requirement|What Prop 65 Does|Key Impact|
|---|---|---|
|**List Maintenance**|Requires California to maintain and regularly update a list of chemicals known to the state to cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm. (The list currently has over 900 chemicals.)|Provides a definitive, enforceable list of hazardous substances.|
|**Warning Requirement**|Requires businesses to provide a **"clear and reasonable warning"** before knowingly and intentionally exposing anyone to a listed chemical.|**Informs consumers** about potential chemical exposure in products, homes, and workplaces (e.g., labels on packaging, signs in buildings).|
|**Discharge Prohibition**|Prohibits businesses from knowingly discharging a significant amount of a listed chemical into sources of drinking water.|**Protects drinking water** quality across the state.|
|**Enforcement**|Allows the California Attorney General, District Attorneys, City Attorneys, and private citizens acting in the public interest to sue companies for non-compliance.|Provides strong **incentive for businesses** to remove or reduce listed chemicals or provide clear warnings.|
|**Application**|Applies to all companies doing business in California with **10 or more employees**.|Affects a wide range of products, services, and environments, from retail goods to dental offices.|
