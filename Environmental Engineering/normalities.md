#environmental
The normality of a solution is the measure of solution concentrate compared to the solute per liter of solution, in short, equivalent per liter
$$
\frac{eq}{L}
$$
or milliequivalent per liter
$$
\frac{meq}{L}
$$
Easy way to calculate this is just to multiply the Molarity by the charge of the ion

So $Mg^{+2}$ with a concentration of $3\frac{\text{mol}}{\text{L}}$ would have a normality of 2 times 3, or $6 \frac{\text{eq}}{L}$
