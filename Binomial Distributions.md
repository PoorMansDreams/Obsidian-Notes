Consider there is 1 stoplight between my house and my destination, the probability that I stop as a stoplight is $p$. Let $X$ be the number of times I stop on my trip.

There are only two possibilities, 
$X=0$ when $f(x)=1-p$
$X=1$ when $f(x)=p$ 

So the times stopped being zero means that you **avoided** the probability $p$ which is the complement of $p$ which is $1-p$
