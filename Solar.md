Solar is powered by the sun, zero emissions, little maintenance once installed can last a lifetime, for every 1 million dollars invested, 5-15 jobs are created.

Downsides: expensive to maintain, doesn't work at night, hard to store energy, isn't completely efficient, doesn't utilize all of the suns energy yet.

Two basic catagories of solar energy, 
1. Solar Thermal - using the suns energy to heat stuff
2. Solar electric - Turning light from the sun into electricity.

Optimum Solar collection depends on the season and the location of the planet. 

Solar can also be used for creation of compost
